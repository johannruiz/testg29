Before using force feedback

This page can command 100% of the device's requested force. Sudden steering forces may cause injury or hardware damage. Fastening the wheel, clearing hands for initial tests, and starting at a low force are strongly recommended. Do not test with children near the wheel.

The tester is experimental and has not been validated on real hardware here. Automatic stopping is best-effort: a frozen browser cannot guarantee that a device motor stops. If the wheel behaves unexpectedly, disconnect its power.

2 · Force limits & timing

Full 0–100% adjustment is available. Changes affect the next effect and live L1/R1 tests. Individual finite-duration tests automatically stop.

3 · Fixed rumble & L1/R1

L1 uses the weak level, R1 the strong level; holding both alternates them. “Rumble” means oscillating steering torque, not separate gamepad vibration motors.

Effects are turned off on release, connection loss, page blur or stale input. ESC is the emergency stop key.

5 · Auto-center
