2. Test force feedback

Safety: Secure the wheel, keep hands clear during the first test, and use its power adapter. A G29 can register steering and buttons over USB while the FFB motors lack power. This page caps test force to 12%.

EffectIdle

The G29 does not have two typical gamepad rumble motors. Here, “rumble” is simulated using short, alternating steering forces. No USB mode-changing commands are sent.
